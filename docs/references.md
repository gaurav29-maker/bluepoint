## Vibe-Trading — https://github.com/HKUDS/Vibe-Trading

An open-source (MIT) agent framework from HKUDS. LLM-driven: it automates
investment research and strategy backtesting, and executes live trades through
broker integrations. Python and FastAPI with a React front end, 25+ market
data sources, 14+ brokers. Actively maintained, and presented as a production
framework rather than a research demo.

**Filed here, and deliberately not anywhere else.**

Bluepoint's positioning is that a *person* reads your portfolio. The site says
so in several places that were written on purpose:

- *Not an automated score. A person reads your portfolio, not a model.*
- The FAQ answers "Is this AI reading my portfolio?" with "No."
- *Nothing is executed for you, and no one follows up to sell you anything.*

An LLM agent that researches and trades is the exact category the product
defines itself against, so it cannot appear in the customer-facing product,
on the marketing site, or as a dependency without contradicting the thing the
brand is built on.

**Where it could legitimately fit.** If the internal research team happens,
its tooling is a separate question from what the customer is sold. A
researcher using automated screening or backtesting to *inform* their own work
is not the same as an algorithm handing a verdict to a customer — in the same
way an analyst using Excel does not make the output "an Excel recommendation".
Two conditions if it goes that way:

1. **It stays internal.** Nothing generated by it reaches a customer as
   output. What reaches a customer is what a named expert says, having done
   the thinking themselves.
2. **The execution half stays off.** Bluepoint executes nothing for anybody.
   Broker integrations that place trades are a different business with a
   different licence, and turning them on would change what the platform *is*
   under SEBI, not merely what it can do.

Worth a conversation with the lawyer before it touches anything, alongside the
research-team question — the two are the same question.
